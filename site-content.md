## Design & Style Guide (final direction)

**Overall feel:** warm, bold, editorial personal-brand site. One accent color family used consistently — no rainbow of card colors, no unrelated hues sprinkled in.

**Palette (final):**
- Background/base: warm cream (`#FBF3E9`)
- Headline/primary text: warm charcoal (`#2A2118`)
- Body/secondary text: muted warm brown-gray (`#6B5F4F`)
- Accent (single family, used everywhere): terracotta — light tint `#F3E4DC` for small cards/icon backgrounds, mid tone `#C1502E` for links/small stat numbers/icons, deep shade `#9E3D1F` for the large hero stat block, darkest `#4A1B0C` for text sitting on the light tint
- No green, no blue, no unrelated hues — everything resolves to cream + charcoal + the terracotta family

**Hero:**
- Cream background block, rounded corners
- Small eyebrow line ("Hey, I'm Charlene") in mid-terracotta
- Large bold two-line statement headline as the emotional hook — first line in charcoal, second line (the punchy word) in mid-terracotta. E.g. "I hate" (charcoal) / "inefficiency." (terracotta)
- Supporting line below in muted brown-gray: the "8 years, 5 countries" bio hook
- Two buttons side by side: solid charcoal primary ("See my work"), outlined charcoal secondary ("Download CV")

**Stat row (directly below hero):**
- Asymmetric layout, not a plain 3-column row: one large deep-terracotta block (2/3 width) for the single most impressive stat (e.g. "150+ users onboarded"), stacked with a smaller light-tint card on the right (1/3 width) split into two mini stats (e.g. "100+ team scaled", "70% time saved") each with a small terracotta icon
- Text on the deep-terracotta block uses cream/light-tint color; text on the light-tint cards uses the darkest terracotta shade

**Skills:** 3-column row of simple cards, light-tint terracotta icon background, bold title, one-line muted description. No hand-drawn badges.

**Work experience (CV):** vertical timeline, terracotta dot for the current/most recent role, muted gray dots for earlier roles, thin connecting line. Company name bold charcoal, role/location/dates in muted brown-gray, 1–2 line summary beneath.

**Work & Project:** uniform card grid — same light neutral/cream card style across all cards (not one different color per card), placeholder/image area on top, project name + tags below.

**Testimonials:** centered section heading, 3-column layout, small initials-avatar circle (terracotta light tint, mid-terracotta text) above each quote, quote in muted brown-gray, name in smaller muted text below.

**Typography:** clean sans-serif throughout. Large, bold, confident headline treatment for the hero statement specifically (this is the one "loud" typographic moment on the site) — everything else stays clean and restrained.

**Overall principle:** restraint plus one bold moment. One background color, one text-color pair, one accent family — but the hero headline is allowed to be big and emotionally punchy since it's the single focal point of the page.
