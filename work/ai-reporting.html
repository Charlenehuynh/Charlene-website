Case Study

AI Reporting Automation (Microsoft Power Automate)

Power Automate Jira Integration AI/LLM Prompts Workflow Optimization
Weekly Dev Progress Report
✓Recurrence
✓List rows present in a table
✓Apply to each
✓Run a prompt (AI)
✓Send an email

Challenge

Preparing the weekly progress report meant 2+ hours going through stand-up logs, Jira tickets, and chasing developers to translate technical updates into business language.

Solution

Built a Friday-morning automation: Power Automate gathers the week's completed Jira tickets and stand-up logs, feeds them into an AI engine with a custom prompt that translates engineering jargon into business language, then emails a polished summary automatically.

Result

Turned a four-hour task into a zero-minute process — developers stay focused on code, stakeholders get a high-quality update.
