Case Study

AI Reporting Automation (Microsoft Power Automate)

A four-hour weekly reporting grind, replaced by an AI summary that writes and sends itself every Friday morning.

01

The Problem

Hours lost translating updates by hand

Every week, preparing the progress report meant 2+ hours of my own time going through daily stand-up logs and Jira tickets — plus more spent chasing developers to translate technical updates into business-ready language. All in, close to a four-hour headache.

02

The Build

A Friday-morning flow that writes the report itself

Every Friday morning, Power Automate automatically pulls the week's completed Jira tickets (via the Jira connector) and daily stand-up logs, feeding them into an AI engine. A custom prompt I designed translates the engineering jargon into clear business language, and the system instantly generates a polished weekly summary — including blockers and next steps — and emails it straight to my inbox.

The Result

Zero-minute reporting, every Friday

We turned that four-hour weekly headache into a zero-minute process. Developers stay focused on their code, and stakeholders get a high-quality update every Friday without anyone chasing anyone.
