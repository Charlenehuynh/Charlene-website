Case Study

Weekly Business Review Automation (Power Query)

Three messy weekly sources, combined into one team and market report that updates with a single refresh.

01

The Problem

Three sources, copied by hand every week

Every week there was a Business Review where I had to create a report and present how my team and the whole market were doing. Building it meant copying by hand from three different kinds of source. It took over an hour each week, and the layout broke whenever someone edited a sheet.

Google Sheets · daily

Agents' call trackers

1st and 2nd successful calls per day, customer book and target per agent, one block per team.

AgentMonTueAgent 181OFF

QA team's file · monthly

Quality scores

Up to 4 tests per agent per month and a total average, for every agent in the market.

AgentTest 1AvgAgent 1896%95.2%

Tableau export · weekly

Solution actions

Solutions pitched and actioned per agent, with extra columns and unknown rows.

AgentPitchedRateAgent 18823.7%

The trackers couldn't be trusted to add up either: days off, empty customer books and targets, and numbers typed as text, which the sheet's own totals silently skipped.

02

The Build

Five steps from raw files to finished report

To automate this, here's what I did. Usually each team's tracker, the QA file and the Tableau export are separate files, so I just save the new ones into one folder and Power Query picks them all up. For this demo I put everything in one workbook so it's portable.

Then five Power Query steps run in order every time I click Refresh:

  1. Clean the call trackers Tracker_Clean

    Reads agents and team leads, drops TOTAL and empty rows, turns daily columns into rows, and fixes days off and numbers typed as text.

  2. Build one row per agent Agent_Report

    Adds up each agent's calls and joins their QA score and Tableau solutions by name.

  3. Roll up by team and market Market_Report

    Totals each team and the whole market against the target row.

  4. Pick out my team My_Team

    Shows my agents one by one; change the team lead's name to see another team.

  5. Prepare the weekly charts Weekly

    Week-by-week coverage against target, for the charts in the slide.

Rules built in, agreed with the team:

  • Recalculate, don't trust. Totals are rebuilt from the raw days, not taken from the sheet's formulas.
  • Documented defaults. An empty book counts as 150 customers and an empty target as 80% of the book, agreed with the team, instead of silently becoming zero.
  • Weight rates properly. A team's solution rate is total actioned ÷ total pitched, not an average of agents' rates, so 300 pitches count for more than 60.

Why Power Query, not VBA? Importing and reshaping the same three files every week is repeatable and standard, so a one-click refresh fits. Branching judgment rules, like the ERP data clean-up, suit VBA better.

03

The Result

One refresh, ready for the weekly slide

Data › Refresh All now produces my team agent by agent, the market team by team, each KPI green or red against target, plus the coverage charts that went into the weekly slide.
