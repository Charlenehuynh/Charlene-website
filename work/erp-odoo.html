Case Study

ERP Architecture & Business Process Mapping (Odoo)

Odoo ERP Business Process Mapping Workflow Integration

Challenge

A 50-person telecom business relied on fragmented software and manual processes, with scattered data and limited visibility between sales, installation, and accounting.

Solution

Designed and implemented a centralized Odoo ERP structure, mapping end-to-end workflow connecting CRM, Sales, Project Management, and Accounting.

Result

Everything in one place — 150+ monthly invoices automated, reduced manual data entry, clearer cross-department visibility.
